## Instructions

Vague prompts get vague answers. When you ask an AI assistant for something, it fills
gaps with guesses — and its guesses are rarely the ones you had in mind.

In this lesson you'll practice turning a vague request into a specific one, by naming:

- **What you want produced** (a list, a paragraph, a table, code, a decision)
- **For whom** (a beginner, an expert, a specific audience)
- **Any constraints** (length, format, tone, things to avoid)

You don't need more words — you need the right details.

## Practice

Below is a vague prompt. Rewrite it as a specific prompt using the three elements
above.

> Vague prompt: "Tell me about project management."

Write your rewritten prompt in the box below, then mark yourself done when you're happy
with it.

## Feedback criteria

A strong rewrite:

1. Specifies the output format (e.g. "a 5-bullet summary", "a comparison table").
2. Names an audience or use case (e.g. "for a first-time team lead").
3. Adds at least one concrete constraint (length, tone, what to exclude).
4. Is still one or two sentences — specific, not bloated.

If your rewrite is missing one of these, that's exactly the gap this lesson is
practicing — try again before moving on.
