## Instructions

An AI assistant has no memory of your team, your project, or your usual standards
unless you give it that context. And one example is often worth several sentences of
explanation.

In this lesson you'll practice adding two things to a prompt:

- **Context**: the background the assistant needs but doesn't have (who this is for,
  what already exists, what's already been tried).
- **An example**: a sample of the kind of output you want, even a rough one.

## Practice

Take this prompt and add both context and a short example to it:

> Base prompt: "Write a Slack message announcing that the sprint deadline moved to
> next Friday."

Write your improved version below, including at least one made-up but plausible detail
of context and one example line the message could open with. Mark yourself done when
you're happy with it.

## Feedback criteria

A strong rewrite:

1. Adds at least one concrete piece of context (audience, channel, prior situation)
   that changes how the message should read.
2. Includes an example — even one line — of the tone or opening you want.
3. Doesn't just repeat the base prompt with extra adjectives; the added detail should
   actually change what a good answer looks like.

If the example and context don't change the shape of the answer, add a more specific
one.
